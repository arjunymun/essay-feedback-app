# DraftLens Trust-First Smoke Grader

A passing review should:

- Preserve schema validity for every report.
- Flag fake DOI or fabricated-metadata cases as unsafe to trust.
- Avoid treating citation metadata as proof that a claim is supported.
- Use uncertainty language when evidence is partial or missing.
- Label rewrite suggestions as optional and non-authoritative.
- Produce trace metadata with ingestion, claim extraction, verification, synthesis, and critic steps.
